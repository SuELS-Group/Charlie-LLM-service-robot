The Admissions Office, called Immatrikulationsamt or I-Amt, handles applications, admission, enrolment and student status; it is on the first floor of A12.
International applicants can contact admission.bachelor@uol.de for bachelor's admission or admission.master@uol.de for master's admission.
The International Office's Welcome Service helps with orientation and life in Oldenburg; it is a separate service from the Admissions Office.
