New international students can use the University of Oldenburg's checklist for organising their stay to find the relevant contacts for housing, enrolment, insurance, city registration and residence matters.
The Welcome Service at welcome@uol.de and the international tutors at international.tutor@uol.de help with first orientation in Oldenburg.
The Welcome Week introduces the university, the city, other students and important formalities; current programmes are at https://uol.de/en/welcome-week.
