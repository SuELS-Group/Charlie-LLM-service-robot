The CaféBar is in the ground-floor foyer of A12, the Student Service Centre; opening hours should be checked with Studierendenwerk Oldenburg.
Studierendenwerk's BAföG service, student-finance advice, social advice and disability counselling are also listed on the ground floor of A12.
For those social-support and financial-counselling services, the destination from the IO waiting area is floor 0, rather than an IO staff office on floor 3.
