Admission to the University of Oldenburg does not include a guaranteed room, and the university does not operate its own student halls of residence.
Studierendenwerk Oldenburg operates student residences; applications and availability must be checked with Studierendenwerk, and private accommodation is another option.
For the university's housing guide, use https://uol.de/en/study-in-oldenburg/organise-your-stay/finding-accomodation.
The IO and international tutors can provide orientation for a housing search, but cannot promise a room or immediate vacancy.
