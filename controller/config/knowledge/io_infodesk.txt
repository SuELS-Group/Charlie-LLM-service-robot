The SSC InfoDesk is in the ground-floor foyer of A12; from the IO waiting area on the third floor, the destination is floor 0.
The InfoDesk provides initial advice, consultation tickets and routing to the appropriate university service.
The published SSC pages give inconsistent general InfoDesk opening hours, so confirm current availability through the InfoService at +49 441 798-2728 or studium@uol.de.
The separately published IO consultation and waiting-ticket times identify when to obtain an IO consultation ticket.
