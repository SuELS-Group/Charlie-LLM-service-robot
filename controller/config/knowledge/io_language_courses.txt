The University of Oldenburg's Language Centre, called Sprachenzentrum, offers German courses for international students, other language courses, placement information and language tests.
The Language Centre's administration office can be reached at +49 441 798-4460; course registration and current offers are at https://uol.de/en/school3/language-centre.
The International Office's advice about a stay abroad does not itself enrol a visitor in a Language Centre course.
