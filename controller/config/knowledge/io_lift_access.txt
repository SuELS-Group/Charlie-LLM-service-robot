The official Haarentor accessibility plan marks a lift and an accessible entrance for the Student Service Centre, building A12.

The available campus plan does not establish the exact corridor route from Charlie's waiting-area station to the lift, or whether the lift is working right now.
For the precise accessible route or a lift fault, ask the SSC InfoDesk or IO staff.
