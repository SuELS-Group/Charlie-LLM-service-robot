For people living within the City of Oldenburg, home-address registration is handled by the city's Bürgerbüro, and residence-permit applications or renewals by its Foreign Residents' Office, including the Hochschulservice.
The City of Oldenburg's Hochschulservice contact is hochschulservice@stadt-oldenburg.de.
The International Office provides orientation about these procedures, but does not issue residence permits or city-registration certificates.
For individual requirements, fees and appointments, consult the responsible city service; the IO Welcome Service at welcome@uol.de can help identify the right contact.
