For International Office consultations at Oldenburg's Student Service Centre, the InfoDesk clarifies your advice topic and issues a paper waiting ticket with a QR code.
Scanning this consultation ticket shows how many visitors are ahead of you and current waiting-list information, even away from the SSC reception desk.
Students of the University of Oldenburg can instead check in for counselling with their CampusCard and view or cancel their consultation ticket in Stud.IP.
The number of visitors ahead of you does not establish an exact waiting time in minutes.
